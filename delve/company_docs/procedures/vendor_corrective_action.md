# Vendor Corrective Action Procedure

| Field   | Value                      |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Date    | 2026-03-09                 |
| Version | 1.0                        |
| Owner   | Mike Levene (Head of Ops)  |

## Purpose

Define the process for addressing findings that arise during vendor assessments, ensuring vendors remediate issues or that appropriate action is taken.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the Finding

During a vendor assessment (annual or quarterly), a finding is identified. Examples:
- Expired or missing SOC 2 report
- Failed security control or policy gap
- Data handling concern (e.g., data retained beyond agreed terms)
- Non-compliance with contractual security requirements

### 2. Document and Notify

Document the finding with specifics (what was found, which requirement it relates to, severity). Notify the vendor in writing (email is sufficient).

### 3. Request a Remediation Plan

Ask the vendor to provide a remediation plan that includes:
- Specific actions they will take
- Timeline for completion
- Point of contact for follow-up

### 4. Track Remediation

Monitor the vendor's progress against the agreed timeline. Follow up at regular intervals (e.g., every 2 weeks for high-severity findings).

### 5. Escalate if Unresolved

If the vendor fails to remediate within the agreed timeline, escalate to the CISO (Alex Kumamoto) and CEO (Raghav Bansal) for a decision:
- **Implement additional compensating controls** on our side
- **Renegotiate the contract** with stronger security requirements
- **Terminate the vendor relationship** and migrate to an alternative

### 6. Document the Outcome

Record the final outcome: remediation completed, compensating controls added, contract changed, or vendor terminated. Update the vendor inventory with current status.
