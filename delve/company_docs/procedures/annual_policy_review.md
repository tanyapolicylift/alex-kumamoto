## Purpose

Ensure all security policies, charters, and the company handbook are reviewed at least annually for accuracy, relevance, and compliance.

## Procedure

### 1. Schedule the Review

CISO schedules the annual policy review cycle. Suggested timing: January each year, to align with the start of the calendar year.

### 2. Review All Policies and Charters

The following documents are reviewed for accuracy and relevance:

- Information Security Policy
- Access Control and Termination Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Change Management Policy
- Data Classification Policy
- Incident Response Policy
- Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy
- Network Security Policy
- Baseline Hardening Policy
- Personnel Security Policy
- Vendor Management Policy
- Risk Assessment and Treatment Policy
- CISO Policy
- PolicyLift Handbook
- Board of Directors Charter
- RGEC Charter
- ITLC Charter

### 3. Evaluate Each Policy

For each document:
- Confirm it is still accurate and reflects current operations.
- Note any updates needed (regulatory changes, tooling changes, org changes).
- If changes are needed, follow the Policy Change Process.
- Update the version number and date, even if no substantive changes were made (to record the review).

### 4. ITLC Approval

Submit the reviewed policies to the ITLC for approval. Batch approval is acceptable -- all policies can be approved in a single review session.

### 5. Annual Personnel Acknowledgment

All personnel re-acknowledge the full set of policies as part of the annual cycle. Track acknowledgment completion for audit evidence.

### 6. Document the Review

For each policy, record:
- Date of review
- Reviewer
- Changes made, or "No changes -- confirmed accurate"

### 7. Store Records

Save review documentation in the compliance evidence repository for audit purposes.
