## Purpose

Define the process for proposing, reviewing, approving, and communicating changes to security policies.

## Procedure

### 1. Draft the Change

The proposer drafts the policy modification, clearly marking what is being added, removed, or revised.

### 2. CTO/CISO Review

Alex Kumamoto (CTO/CISO) reviews the draft and either approves or requests revisions. Iterate until the change is acceptable.

### 3. ITLC Approval

The IT Leadership Committee gives final approval. This can be done:
- **Async via Slack** for non-urgent or minor changes
- **In the quarterly ITLC meeting** for substantive changes

### 4. Version and Date the Policy

- Increment the version number (e.g., 1.0 to 1.1 for minor, 1.x to 2.0 for major).
- Update the "Last Revised" date.
- Add a brief entry to the policy's revision history.

### 5. Notify All Personnel

Announce the change via Slack and/or email. Include a summary of what changed and a link to the updated policy.

### 6. Maintain a Change Record

Document:
- What changed (summary)
- Who proposed and approved the change
- Date of approval

### 7. Personnel Acknowledgment (Material Changes)

If the change is material (new obligations, significant scope changes), require all personnel to acknowledge the updated policy. Track acknowledgment for audit evidence.
