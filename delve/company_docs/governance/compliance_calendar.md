## Purpose

This calendar tracks all recurring review, assessment, and meeting obligations from PolicyLift's SOC 2 policies. Tasks are staggered across the year to distribute workload evenly.

---

## Suggested Annual Timeline

**Q1 (January -- March)**
- January: Annual policy review, Q1 quarterly access review, Q1 quarterly vendor assessment, Q1 ITLC meeting
- February: Annual risk assessment, firewall rule review, network diagram review
- March: Security awareness training, code of conduct reaffirmation

**Q2 (April -- June)**
- April: Annual access review, Q2 quarterly access review, Q2 quarterly vendor assessment, Q2 ITLC meeting, RGEC meeting (spring)
- May: Annual penetration test
- June: Annual vendor risk re-assessment

**Q3 (July -- September)**
- July: Annual insurance coverage review, Q3 quarterly access review, Q3 quarterly vendor assessment, Q3 ITLC meeting
- August: Incident response plan test / tabletop
- September: BC/DR plan test, backup restoration test

**Q4 (October -- December)**
- October: Q4 quarterly access review, Q4 quarterly vendor assessment, Q4 ITLC meeting, RGEC meeting (fall)
- November: Board meeting
- December: (Buffer month -- catch up on any outstanding items)
