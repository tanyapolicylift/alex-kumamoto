## Responsibilities

The Board is responsible for:

- Oversight of the company's risk management and security posture
- Reviewing and approving the annual risk assessment
- Receiving regular reports from the CISO on security matters
- Ensuring adequate resources are allocated to the security and compliance program
- Reviewing audit findings and remediation plans
