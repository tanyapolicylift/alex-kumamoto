## Purpose

This document provides brief descriptions of each security-relevant role at PolicyLift. These descriptions define the scope of responsibility for personnel whose duties directly affect the company's security and compliance posture.

---

## Role Descriptions

### Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) -- Alex Kumamoto

Alex Kumamoto serves as PolicyLift's CISO, responsible for defining and executing the company-wide security strategy. This includes ownership of all security and compliance policies, oversight of the risk management program, management of the security awareness training program, vendor security oversight, and incident response program leadership. The CISO manages the compliance calendar, coordinates with external auditors, and maintains the security budget. The CISO reports directly to the CEO (Raghav Bansal) and provides regular updates to the Board of Directors.

---

### Chief Technology Officer (CTO) -- Alex Kumamoto

Alex Kumamoto also serves as PolicyLift's CTO, responsible for the company's technology strategy, production system architecture, and infrastructure security. The CTO holds policy modification authority, meaning all changes to security and compliance policies require CTO review and approval. The CTO oversees change management processes for production systems, maintains the network architecture and firewall configurations, and serves as the BC/DR Incident Commander. The CTO manages the engineering organization and sets technical standards for the company.

---

### Chief Executive Officer (CEO) -- Raghav Bansal

Raghav Bansal serves as CEO and provides executive oversight of the security and compliance program. The CEO holds final risk acceptance authority for risks that exceed the CISO's tolerance thresholds. As Board Chairman, the CEO ensures that security and risk matters receive appropriate board-level attention. The CEO is the primary external point of contact for audit engagements and regulatory inquiries, and is responsible for ensuring the company allocates adequate resources to its security program.

---

### Chief Engineering Architect -- Yurii Kabannik

Yurii Kabannik serves as PolicyLift's Chief Engineering Architect, responsible for system architecture decisions, infrastructure design, and production environment management. Yurii is the designated BC/DR Technical Recovery Lead, responsible for restoring production systems and validating data integrity during disaster recovery events. This role includes oversight of deployment pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, cloud environment configuration, and backup systems. Yurii reports to the CTO.

---

### Head of Operations -- Mike Levene

Mike Levene serves as PolicyLift's Head of Operations, responsible for day-to-day operational processes that support the security and compliance program. This includes vendor management (onboarding assessments, periodic re-assessments, contract reviews), HR compliance processes (background checks, onboarding/offboarding checklists, policy reaffirmation tracking), and insurance coverage coordination. Mike is the designated BC/DR Communications Lead, responsible for internal and external communications during business continuity events. Mike reports to the CEO.
