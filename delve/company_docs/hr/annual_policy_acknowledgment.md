## Purpose

All PolicyLift employees must complete this acknowledgment annually. It confirms that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with all current company policies. Completed acknowledgments must be retained for the audit period (minimum 12 months, recommended to retain for duration of employment plus 1 year).

---

## Acknowledgment Text

I, [Employee Name], confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to comply with the following PolicyLift policies:

- Information Security Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Data Classification Policy
- Code of Business Conduct and Ethics
- Employee Handbook (including Sanctions Policy)

I understand that violation of these policies may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

I understand my obligation to report any suspected security incidents immediately via the #security-incidents Slack channel or to my manager.

Signature: _______________

Date: _______________
