## Purpose

This is NOT a full employee handbook. It is a checklist of sections and language that our SOC 2 policies require the handbook to contain. The team should verify that each section exists in the current handbook (via our PEO/HR platform) and add any that are missing.

---

## Required Handbook Sections

- [ ] **Sanctions / Disciplinary Policy** -- States that violations of security policies, code of conduct, or company policies may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. (Personnel Security #5, AUP #8)

- [ ] **Progressive Discipline Process** -- Outlines escalation: verbal warning, written warning, suspension, termination (or similar graduated approach). (PolicyLift Handbook #3)

- [ ] **Anti-Harassment Policy** -- Includes complaint procedure and investigation process. (PolicyLift Handbook #4)

- [ ] **Acceptable Use of Technology** -- Summarizes or references the Acceptable Use Policy. Includes: no personal email for business use, report security incidents immediately, do not tamper with logs. (PolicyLift Handbook #5, AUP)

- [ ] **Confidentiality** -- Summarizes obligations around company and customer information. References the full Confidentiality/IP Agreement. (PolicyLift Handbook #6)

- [ ] **Security Incident Reporting** -- How to report: #security-incidents Slack channel. What to report: suspected breaches, lost devices, credential compromises, unusual system behavior, policy violations. (AUP #7, Incident Response #9)

- [ ] **Policy Change Notification** -- Company will notify employees of material policy changes. (PolicyLift Handbook #8)

- [ ] **Acknowledgment Page** -- Employee signs confirming receipt and understanding of the handbook. (PolicyLift Handbook #1)
