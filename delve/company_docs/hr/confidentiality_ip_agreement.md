## Purpose

This outline specifies the sections and provisions that must be included in PolicyLift's Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement. The final agreement will be signed by all employees and contractors before they are granted access to company systems or data.

---

## Required Sections

### 1. Definition of Confidential Information

Broad definition covering at minimum:

- Business data and plans
- Customer data and personally identifiable information
- Technical information (source code, architecture, infrastructure details)
- Financial data
- Product strategies and roadmaps
- Security configurations and practices
- Any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential

### 2. Non-Disclosure Obligations

- Obligation not to disclose Confidential Information to any third party without authorization
- Obligation to use Confidential Information only for performing job duties
- Obligations apply during and after employment or engagement
- Permitted disclosures (e.g., as required by law, with prior written consent)

### 3. IP Assignment Clause

- All work product created during the course of employment or engagement is the sole property of PolicyLift
- Includes inventions, code, designs, documentation, and any other intellectual property
- Employee/contractor waives moral rights where applicable
- Prior inventions exclusion list (employee may list pre-existing IP they wish to exclude)

### 4. Return of Materials Upon Termination

- All company materials, documents, devices, and data must be returned upon termination
- All copies of Confidential Information must be deleted from personal devices and accounts
- Applies to both physical and electronic materials

### 5. Non-Solicitation (Optional -- Per Legal Counsel)

- Restriction on soliciting PolicyLift employees or customers for a defined period after departure
- Scope and duration to be determined by legal counsel based on applicable state law

### 6. Acknowledgment of Company Policies

- Employee/contractor acknowledges receipt and understanding of:
  - Information Security Policy
  - Acceptable Use Policy
- Agreement to comply with all company security and data handling policies

### 7. Surviving Obligations

- Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment or engagement
- Duration of survival to be specified by legal counsel (common: indefinite for trade secrets, 2-5 years for other confidential information)
