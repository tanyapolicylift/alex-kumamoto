## Decision Format

When logging a decision, create a new file: `YYYY-MM-DD-decision-title.md`

```markdown
---
created: YYYY-MM-DD
author: Name

decision-maker: Name
tags: [CRM, decision]
---

# Decision: [Title]

What situation led to this decision?

## Options Considered
1. **Option A**: Description, pros/cons
2. **Option B**: Description, pros/cons

## Decision
What was decided and why.

## Consequences
- What changes as a result
- What we're accepting/trading off

```
