**Cross-check:** A typical personal lines producer processes 3-5 quotes per day. An agency with 3-5 producers generates 12-25 quotes/day or ~3,000-6,500 quotes/year. This aligns with the EZLynx data.

- ~150-200M quotes nationally produce ~30-50M new/renewal policies through IAs
- Each quote typically involves 3-8 carrier comparisons, so actual carrier submissions = **450M-1.6B carrier rate requests/year**

## 6. Market Dynamics

### 6A. Homeowners Insurance Crisis -- Opportunity Creation

**California:**
- **7 of top 12 insurers** have limited new policies or paused writing since 2022
- State Farm: 17% emergency rate hike approved (June 2025); non-renewed thousands of policies
- **FAIR Plan** policies grew from 235K (2018) to **452K (2024)** -- 92% increase
- FAIR Plan exposure: $458B (up from $153B in 2020)
- January 2025 LA wildfires: 12,000+ structures destroyed; $914M+ in FAIR Plan payouts
- **Opportunity:** Carriers leaving creates demand for IAs to find coverage across remaining/new markets. More carriers = more need for multi-carrier quoting technology.

**Florida:**
- **17 new carriers** entered market since 2022 reforms
- **30+ carriers** have returned or expanded
- Citizens depopulated **546,000 policies** in 2025 alone (from 936K to 395K)
- Homeowners lawsuits down 30%; Citizens lawsuits down 50%
- **Opportunity:** Depopulation pushes policies to private carriers distributed through IAs. More carrier options = more need for rating technology. New entrants need distribution through IAs.

**Cross-state opportunity:** The homeowners crisis is INCREASING the value of independent agents and multi-carrier technology because:
1. Consumers need help navigating shrinking carrier availability
2. New/specialty carriers entering need IA distribution
3. E&S/surplus lines growing in PL -- agents need access to non-standard markets
4. Rate volatility means shopping is critical -- comparative rating saves consumers money

## Key Takeaways for Product Strategy

1. **Homeowners is the beachhead.** IAs control 52% of homeowners distribution vs. 33% of auto. The homeowners crisis in CA and FL is creating urgent demand.

2. **The rating market is a duopoly ripe for disruption.** Applied Systems (EZLynx + Applied Rater) and Roper (Vertafore PL Rating) control ~85% of comparative rating. Agencies express frustration with pricing and integration.

3. **4 target states = 35% of national HO market and 45-50% of IA agencies.** High concentration makes geographic focus efficient.

4. **Quote volume is massive.** 150-200M personal lines quotes annually through IAs, with 450M-1.6B individual carrier rate requests.

5. **Crisis = opportunity.** CA/FL carrier disruption is forcing agents to work with more carriers, increasing the need for multi-carrier quoting technology. New carrier entrants need efficient IA distribution.

6. **Carrier willingness to pay.** Carriers subsidize rating technology to gain distribution. New/smaller carriers in FL and specialty carriers entering CA are highly motivated to get listed in comparative raters.

7. **Agency consolidation drives technology adoption.** As agencies professionalize and larger aggregators roll up smaller shops, technology infrastructure becomes table stakes.
