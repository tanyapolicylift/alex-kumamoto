# California Workers' Compensation Insurance Carrier Landscape

Deep research on the leading workers' compensation insurance carriers in California, compiled February 2025 from NAIC, California DOI, WCIRB, AM Best, Insurance Journal, and industry sources.

---

## 1. Total California Workers' Comp Market Size

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **CA WC Direct Written Premium (2024)** | **~$11.7 billion** | CA DOI 2024 Market Share Report |
| **CA WC Earned Premium (2024)** | **~$15.5 billion** | WCIRB Quarterly Experience Report |
| **CA WC Earned Premium (2023)** | **~$15.8 billion** | WCIRB |
| **WCIRB Estimated 2025 Policy Year Premium** | **~$16.4 billion** | CA DIR Assessment Filing |
| **CA Share of National WC Market** | **>20%** (largest state; 2x the next largest) | WCIRB / NAIC |
| **National WC DWP (2024)** | **$57.48 billion** | NAIC 2024 Data |
| **CA WC Loss Ratio (2024)** | **45.88%** | CA DOI |
| **CA WC Combined Ratio (2024)** | **~127%** (highest in 20+ years) | WCIRB |
| **Advisory Pure Premium Rate Change (Sep 2025)** | **+8.7%** approved (WCIRB proposed +11.2%) | CA Insurance Commissioner |

> **Note on DWP vs. Earned Premium:** The $11.7B figure is Direct Written Premium reported to the CA DOI via NAIC filings. The $15.5B earned premium figure from WCIRB is a broader actuarial measure that includes premium development and self-insured adjustments. For carrier market share purposes, DWP is the standard metric.

## 2. Number of Carriers

| Metric | Count | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **Total WCIRB Member Companies** | **400+** | WCIRB (every insurer authorized to transact WC in CA must be a WCIRB member) |
| **Carriers Actively Writing Meaningful Volume** | **~50-80** (estimated) | CA DOI rate comparison lists ~50 carriers; top 25 groups account for majority of premium |
| **Top 10 Carriers' Share of CA WC Market** | **~55-65%** (estimated from available data) | Derived from CA DOI / NAIC data |

While over 400 entities are licensed, the market is concentrated. The top 25 insurance groups represent approximately 78.5% of California's total P&C market. In workers' comp specifically, SCIF alone holds ~8-10%, and the top 10 carriers likely control 55-65% of CA WC premium.

## 5. Key Market Dynamics (2024-2025)

### Profitability Crisis
- California WC combined ratio hit **~127% in 2024**, the highest in 20+ years
- Insurers paying $1.27 for every $1.00 collected
- This is significantly worse than the national WC average
- The industry had enjoyed years of underwriting profits, but California is deteriorating faster

### Cost Drivers
- **Medical inflation**: Medical costs per indemnity claim growing at 7.7% annually (2023-2024) vs. 3.7% (2017-2023)
- **Claim duration**: Injured workers staying off work longer, increasing indemnity costs
- **Cumulative trauma claims**: Running ~3%+ higher in CA than all other states; increasing share of indemnity claims
- **Litigation**: California has among the highest litigated claim rates nationally, driving resolution costs up
- **Mental health claims**: Increasing trend

### Rate Environment
- After a decade of rate decreases, rates are now **increasing**
- First year-over-year charged rate increase in over a decade (2H 2025)
- WCIRB proposed +11.2% advisory pure premium increase; Commissioner approved +8.7% effective Sept 1, 2025
- Market shifting from soft to **hardening**
- Expect continued rate pressure through 2026+

### Implications for Agents/Brokers
- Carriers will tighten underwriting appetites as combined ratios worsen
- Hard-to-place risks may shift toward SCIF and E&S/specialty markets
- Technology and digital quoting increasingly important for small business segment
- Loss control and claims management capabilities becoming key differentiators
- Agents should expect more carrier scrutiny on class codes, experience mods, and loss history
