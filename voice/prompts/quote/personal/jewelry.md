Extends [[quote-information]] base prompt

## Information Collection

1. **Type** — ring, watch, necklace, bracelet, earrings, collection
2. **Description** — materials, gemstones, brand
3. **Value** — replacement or appraised
4. **Appraisal** — professional appraisal docs?
5. **Loss History**
6. **Storage** — worn daily, jewelry box, safe, deposit box
7. **Condition**

## Additional Information

- High-value items often require appraisal.
- Secure storage may get discounts.
- Can be scheduled on homeowners or separate personal articles policy.
