## Information Collection

1. **Business Name**
2. **Public vs Private Company**
3. **Number of Directors & Officers**
4. **Investor/Ownership Structure**
5. **Industry Sector**
6. **Prior D&O/Management Liability Claims** — 5 yrs
7. **Pending/Prior Litigation/Regulatory Actions**
8. **Financial Condition Snapshot**
9. **Outside Directorships**

## Additional Information

- Protects leaders from personal liability.
- Public companies face higher scrutiny.
- Entity coverage may also be included.
